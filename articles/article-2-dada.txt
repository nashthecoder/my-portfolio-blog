I didn't begin with code. I began with a question:

"If I didn't know what menopause was… how many others were silently going through it too?"

The Beginning

I was 49 when the symptoms started: Hot flashes. Night sweats. Insomnia. Mood swings. Forgetfulness. Salt in the fridge. Crying, laughing, withdrawing.

And still — I didn't recognize it as menopause.

I've worked in reproductive health for nearly two decades. But no one had prepared me. Not my doctor. Not my workplace. Not even my mother.

When I first shared my experience — in The Guardian and on the Menopause Buddies podcast — the response was immediate and sobering.

If I had missed the signs… how many other African women were navigating menopause in silence?

Breaking the Silence: MAA-Africa

That's why I started MAA-Africa — Menopause Awareness & Advocacy Africa — a community-first initiative to:
- Break the silence
- Share honest, lived stories
- Build culturally relevant support networks across the continent

But the product builder in me had more questions:
Who am I building this for? If we build it, will they come?

Listening, Not Launching

Inspired by Nadia Odunayo's thoughtful approach to building StoryGraph, I chose to listen before I designed anything.

I created a simple survey. 29 women responded — from Kenya, Nigeria, Uganda, South Africa, Tanzania, Botswana, and Eswatini. Here's what they told me:

"I didn't know what was happening to my body." "No one talks about this in my family or workplace." "I want community, not just content." "I want to be ready. I want to know what to expect."

What I'm Building: Meet DADA

From those voices, DADA was born — a menopause companion app designed with and for African women.

Phase 1: A Community-First MVP

This is the tool I wish I had.

DADA will include:
- A guided onboarding flow to help women understand what to expect
- A safe, moderated forum to share stories and ask questions
- Personalized, culturally-relevant content based on your journey
- Features designed for privacy, ease, and emotional support

Building in Public

I'm sharing every step — from UX to technical tradeoffs — on the Product Engineers' Logs over on Medium.

Weekly product milestones:
- Week 1: Onboarding Flow
- Week 2: Forum Structure
- Week 3: Personalization Logic
- …and more to come
